The Indian Air Force (IAF)officially established on 8 October 1932—is the air arm of the Indian Armed Forces and ranks as the fourth-largest air force in the world. Headquartered in New Delhi, its primary mission is to secure Indian airspace and conduct aerial warfare during conflicts. The IAF operates under the leadership of the Chief of the Air Staff, Air Chief Marshal AP Singh.Core Identity & StructureMotto: Nabhaḥ Spr̥śaṁ Dīptam ("Touch the Sky with Glory"), which is derived from the eleventh chapter of the Bhagavad Gita.Personnel: Over 140,000 active personnel operating across seven strategic and functional commands.Primary Fleet: A diverse inventory of over 1,700 aircraft, including advanced multirole fighters like the Sukhoi Su-30MKI, Dassault Rafale, and the indigenous HAL Tejas.Strategic Lift & Attack: Heavy-lift transport is managed by workhorses like the C-17 Globemaster III, while rotary-wing attack capabilities rely on platforms like the AH-64E Apache.
